# Three-shoot compact foliage group

This component experiment follows the research recommendation to use curated small foliage/shoot structures rather than enlarging individual leaves into canopy carriers. Three twig gestures carry fifteen small closed foliage hints: two paired levels and one terminal hint per shoot. The prior eleven-form compact group remains the control.

The candidate uses final-mesh normals, ordinary shadows, opaque merged geometry and the same low-frequency pigment shader. It does not add a crown-normal guide, density field, alpha cards, billboard, reference image, runtime AI or a species claim. A whole-tree assembly is forbidden until front/reverse/top views show that the group reads as a coherent stylized shoot rather than a sparse bundle of literal leaves.
