## Current state, not product truth

This repository is a first courtyard style study. Its validated desktop configuration is Godot 4.7.1 Forward+ on the hardware named in README. That is lab evidence, not permission to replace the product's Mobile/Vulkan target. Blender source and current captures are implementation evidence, not acceptance targets.

The supplied source illustration is not in this repository. Its absence must remain explicit; a fresh session cannot claim a verified match without access to it. Use the target registration process in the evidence protocol. Do not assume Design-Platform references are the same image.

Historical Blender previews remain useful for comparison but cannot override the actual Godot output or the product's current visual charter. Research may propose reusable 3D assets and techniques; Design-Platform and Cody retain product acceptance authority.
